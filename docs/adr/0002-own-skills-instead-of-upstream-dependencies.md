# devflow owns its skills instead of installing Matt Pocock's and pstack's

Through 1.2, devflow installed 20 of Matt Pocock's skills and 43 of pstack's straight from their repos and only orchestrated them. From 1.3 it ships sixteen skills of its own, adapted from those originals, and has no dependencies. The upstream skills could not be edited, so devflow kept patching around them: a grilling setting to soften an interview that asks until nothing is assumed, a models file because pstack defaults to Grok, a rule to merge tickets that one skill forbids resolving together. The two things users felt most, long interviews and a fresh session per ticket, are written into the upstream text itself. Owning the skills lets a flow ask once, stop once for a go, and build every ticket in one session. The cost is that upstream improvements no longer arrive on their own; they have to be read and ported by hand. Both projects are MIT, and `plugin/CREDITS.md` carries their notices and says which devflow skill came from which original.
